Professional Biography

A Relentless Voice for Legal Justice

I am Advocate Ramesh Kumar Pandey, a dedicated Criminal Defense Advocate actively practicing in the Bagaha Civil Court, West Champaran district of Bihar. With an extensive professional background spanning over 10 years, I have devoted my career to safeguarding the constitutional rights, personal liberties, and statutory protections of individuals facing legal scrutiny.

The criminal justice process can often be daunting and complex for litigants and their families. My legal practice is established upon deep procedural knowledge of the Indian Penal Code (IPC), Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC / BNSS), and special criminal enactments.

Whether it involves urgent regular or anticipatory bail petitions, judicial remands, cross-examinations, witness depositions, or trial representations, I ensure thorough preparation and aggressive advocacy inside the courtroom.
